## How the game should go

- **Sabah's approach:** they will probably defend deep and counter, as they did in keeping the Dortmund game close. Joy-Lance Mickels, with 6 league goals in 8 games, is their main threat.
- **Barcelona's edge:** their attacking quality should create enough to score two or three, even with some rotation after the long trip.
- **Likely pattern:** Barcelona control the ball and pull away after halftime, with both teams scoring.

## Biggest unknown

How many defenders Barcelona get back by 25 November. Andreas Christensen, Jules Koundé, Eric García and Xavi Espart were reported out for the Aston Villa game on 3 November. If Koundé and Christensen return, 0-3 becomes more likely. If the defence stays makeshift, 2-3 is a real possibility.
